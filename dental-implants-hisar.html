Why Choose An Implant

The only tooth replacement that replaces the root, too

  • Looks & feels natural — custom-matched to your bite and smile.
  • Protects surrounding teeth — unlike a bridge, no need to cut down healthy neighbouring teeth.
  • Preserves your jawbone — stimulates bone the way a natural tooth root does, preventing the bone loss that follows tooth loss.
  • Built to last — with good care, implants are designed as a long-term, permanent solution.
  • Eat what you like — a fixed implant restores full chewing function, unlike a removable denture.
  • Single tooth or full mouth — from one missing tooth to a complete implant-supported rehabilitation.
The Implant Process

What to expect, start to finish

Consultation & Scan

Clinical exam and X-rays to assess bone density and plan the ideal implant position.

Treatment Plan

A written plan covering the number of implants needed, timeline and full cost — before you commit.

Implant Placement

The titanium implant is placed under local anaesthesia in a comfortable, ISO-certified setting.

Healing & Crown

After a healing period, a custom crown is fitted — completing a tooth that looks and functions naturally.
