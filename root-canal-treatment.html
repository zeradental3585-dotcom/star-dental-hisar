Root Canal Treatment

Root Canal Treatment in Hisar

A root canal treats infection or damage deep inside a tooth, removing the source of pain and saving the tooth rather than extracting it. Dr. Tarun Kalra, an MDS-qualified specialist, completes most root canal treatments in a single sitting — meaning less time off work and fewer visits to the clinic.
